# PointSwap: Loyalty Points Marketplace

A marketplace where people trade unused loyalty points for rewards they'll actually use. For example, if Chick-fil-A and Starbucks participated, one customer could trade Chick-fil-A points for another customer's Starbucks Stars. Customers get more choice, and participating brands see more visits when customers redeem their new rewards.

## The prototype

`index.html` is a single-file demo (no build step) in black, neon orange and white:

- **Wallet:** balances across six loyalty programs, with an estimated dollar value.
- **Open swaps:** browse and filter offers by program; accept a swap and both balances move at once.
- **List a swap:** choose what you give and what you want; the app suggests a fair amount from each program's typical point value, and holds your points until someone accepts or you cancel.

Run it by opening `index.html` in a browser. Balances are stored only in your browser (localStorage).

Brand names are illustrative; this concept is not affiliated with any loyalty program.
